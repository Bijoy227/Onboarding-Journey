# Ask Caboodle — Starter question library (draft for review)

Draft of 18 October 2026, for review with Constance.

These are 100 questions we believe Caboodle users ask. Once you approve a question, we ask it in Ask Caboodle and a person checks the answer. Checked questions then become worked examples, so similar questions get answered the same right way, and they are offered to users as suggested questions.
Words in braces, such as {retailer} or {period}, are fill-ins: the user picks the value (which retailer, which time period), so one question works for every retailer and every period. "We" and "our" mean the brand.
What we ask of you: mark each question Keep, Change or Drop; fix the wording to how your clients really talk; and add the questions your clients ask that are missing (there is space for them near the end).
The "For" column says who would ask it. Questions marked "Broker (after cross-brand access)" ask across all the brands a brokerage represents at once; that comes in a later release.

## How the fill-ins work

A word in braces is a fill-in. When someone uses the question, they pick the value from a list, and the question then reads in plain words (for example, "Which products shipped the most cases last quarter?"). The fill-ins are:

- **{retailer}**: a retailer, meaning the chain or corporate parent.
- **{banner}**: one of a retailer's store brands.
- **{distributor}**: a distributor such as KeHE or UNFI, or one of the brand's delivery methods.
- **{product}**: one of the brand's products.
- **{category}**: one of the brand's product categories.
- **{region}**: a region as set up in Caboodle.
- **{promotion}**: one of the brand's promotions (a sandbox promotion when the question says "sandbox").
- **{period}**: one of these time periods: last month, last quarter, last year, last 3 months, last 12 months, year to date, this year, next year, next 3 months, last 30 days, last 90 days.
- **{dates}**: a month, a quarter, a year, or a date range the user picks.
